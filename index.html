Value-Add Multifamily · Los Angeles

We find the buildings Los Angeles overlooked and give them their next chapter.

RXD Capital acquires underperforming multifamily properties, renovates them with care for their character, and turns them into safer, better places to live, for residents, for neighborhoods, and for our capital partners.

02 Our approach

Simple, repeatable, and hands-on from acquisition to stabilization.

  1. Underperforming and off-market buildings in high-demand LA neighborhoods, bought well below replacement cost.

  2. New kitchens, baths, facades, courtyards, and security, with the original character kept where it matters.

  3. Lease at market, refinance to return capital to partners, and hold for long-term cash flow.
