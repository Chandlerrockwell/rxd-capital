About
RXD Capital
RXD Capital is a Los Angeles-based real estate investment firm focused on value-add multifamily properties. We target underperforming and off-market small multifamily assets in high-demand neighborhoods across LA and broader Southern California.

Our approach is simple: identify properties with untapped potential, execute thoughtful renovations that respect the character of each neighborhood, and bring these assets to life in a way that elevates the community and delivers strong returns to our investors.

We believe every property has a story that hasn't been told yet. Our job is to find it, unlock it, and create something that residents are proud to call home, neighborhoods benefit from, and our capital partners are rewarded by.
